Lasagna

This is a simple and delicious lasagna recipe.

Ingredients

  • 6 Uncooked lasagna sheets
  • 3 Skinless, boneless chicken breast halves
  • 225g Shredded mozzarella cheese, divided
  • 225g Cream cheese, softened
  • 60ml Hot water
  • 1 cube Chicken bouillon
  • 740g Spaghetti sauce

Instructions

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 175°C.
  2. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Cook lasagna sheets in boiling water, stirring occasionally, until tender yet firm to the bite, about 8 minutes. Drain, rinse with cold water, and set aside.
  3. Meanwhile, place chicken in a large saucepan with enough water to cover; bring to a boil. Cook until chicken is no longer pink and the juices run clear, about 20 minutes. Remove chicken from the saucepan and shred.
  4. Combine shredded chicken, 115g mozzarella cheese, and cream cheese in a large bowl. Dissolve hot water and bouillon in a liquid measure; pour over chicken mixture and stir until well combined.
  5. Spread ⅓ of the spaghetti sauce in the bottom of a 23x33cm baking dish. Cover with ½ of the chicken mixture and top with 3 lasagna sheets; repeat. Top with remaining sauce and sprinkle with remaining 115g mozzarella cheese.
  6. Bake in the preheated oven for 45 minutes.
  7. Serve and enjoy!
