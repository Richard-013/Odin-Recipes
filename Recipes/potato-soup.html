Potato Soup

This is a simple and delicious potato soup recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 6 cups mashed cooked potatoes
  • 2 (14.5 ounce) cans chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup milk

Instructions

  1. In a medium soup pot melt butter over low heat, and saute onions until tender.
  2. Stir in the mashed potatoes, and then slowly add the chicken broth.
  3. Stirring, add milk (use more or less to achieve desired creaminess).
  4. Cook until heated through.
  5. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
